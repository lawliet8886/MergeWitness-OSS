## Five attempts

Recruit five external Node/JavaScript developers after contact authorization. Participation is voluntary: no endorsement, meeting, recording or private source is required. Use [EN](PARTICIPATE.md) / [PT-BR](PARTICIPATE_PT_BR.md) instructions and the [public installation form](https://github.com/lawliet8886/MergeWitness-OSS/issues/new?template=installation-check.yml). GitHub is primary; `gabriel@mergewitness.com.br` is secondary and delivery unverified.

Record OS, Node >=22, npm, Git, actual release/version and archive checksum. Give timing instructions before any download or installation: the attempt begins when the participant opens the participant guide with Node, npm and Git prerequisites ready. Include download, archive hash verification, MergeWitness installation, demo and report comprehension in the attempt. Time missing-prerequisite setup separately and record other pauses. If the guide was opened before prerequisites were ready or timing did not begin at this boundary, record the actual boundary/source and leave any inseparable duration unknown; do not invent a restart or replace missing time with zero. The participant installs in a new directory, runs the synthetic demo, opens the actual returned `reportPath`, identifies the Combined failure and repair result, and explains the separate pricing/cache retention checks. Installation alone is not reproduction.

Target **three unaided reproductions among five external attempts**, in approximately ten minutes. This is an internal research target, not a product promise. Record all outcomes: unaided, assisted, not finished, prerequisites missing. Assistance (including AI or additional guidance) ends unaided eligibility; record timing and nature. Missing durations remain null/unknown, not zero. Prerequisite failures stay visible in the five-attempt denominator; report prerequisite-ready count separately. Before five attempts, report actual counts and the pending target.

Asynchronous durations, comprehension answers and help are **participant-reported** unless independently observed with consent and evidence. A submitted response is evidence of a report, not independent observation of execution. Label each source; never upgrade a report to observed timing. Support learning without rewriting the initial outcome as unaided.

## One genuine case; two concrete returns

Ask about a recent real interaction before offering integration. A case must be supplied by an external participant and separately consented; a synthetic fixture or known public replay cannot replace it. The free first pilot covers **one trusted, reviewed Node repository, one case and at most two technical sessions**, which may be asynchronous exchanges scoped as those sessions. Agree on scope first.

Declare revisions, operation sequence, expected observations, retention requirements, checks/helpers and environment before evaluation. Only `node --test` is supported. Keep private source with the participant; worktrees/subprocesses are not hardened sandboxes. A preexisting branch regression is not an interaction-only failure, and inconclusive execution is not pass. Do not weaken frozen checks. The [own-repository walkthrough](USE_YOUR_REPOSITORY.md) explains the workflow; its educational fixture is source-checkout-only.

With optional follow-up consent, seek **two concrete returns in 7-14 days**: a dated new independent use/result, a new case, or a specific technical question grounded in the attempt. Record each category separately. A question is a return but does not prove second independent use. Stars, compliments and social replies do not count. Missing responses and withdrawals remain visible. No follow-up has been sent.

Record previous workflow, measured or reported effort and source, pilot setup/execution/support effort, real attributable cost, agreed acceptance criterion/result, observed benefit/blocker and recurrence. Unknown cost is null; no price, revenue or willingness to pay is invented. Expand only after evidence of a real case and repeat value; otherwise revise onboarding or audience assumptions.
