# Positioning and validation boundary

MergeWitness currently replays a caller-authored operation-sequence probe across trusted Git snapshots and checks candidate repairs against frozen explicitly declared requirements. It has a synthetic price/cache proof-and-repair example and a labeled corpus. It does not automatically discover every regression or prove arbitrary merges/repairs safe.

[QuietClash](https://github.com/arbade/quietclash) already compares executable behavior across base, branches and combination; its documentation includes async JS/TS and observability limits. Previously indexed [mumei documentation](https://github.com/iroha924/mumei) describes reference-test reruns and guards against weakening checks in Claude Code workflows; current source access could not be verified in this pass. Frozen checks and behavioral comparison are overlapping ideas, not proprietary categories or evidence of originality.

A [scoped executable QuietClash workflow comparison](evidence/COMPARISON_0.2.1.md) records a pinned version, calibration, controls and the two original synthetic histories. It does not cover the whole current thirteen-case source corpus or establish general superiority. QuietClash's automatic target inputs did not exercise the directed operation sequences; no alert is not evidence those sequences are safe. Current mumei source was unavailable in this unauthenticated pass, so its component comparison is explicitly not-run. Keep future comparisons confined to supported scenarios, pinned versions and published commands; report unsupported and inconclusive cases.

The four public published-module incident replays (DataLoader, lru-cache and fastq) validate a technical ability to replay known sequence-sensitive behavior using unchanged published modules in a minimal harness. They do not establish maintainer adoption or autonomous discovery. One initial DataLoader observation was corrected against upstream evidence and its held-out label withdrawn. The fastq #80 oracle was reserved before upstream execution/integration, but issue/fix sources were reviewed; the corpus is not a blind benchmark. The current source corpus adds one minimized concurrent fastq drain-wait variant to those eight synthetic and four published-module entries: thirteen cases total. Its old module is published fastq 1.17.0; its positive candidate is pinned open PR #87 source, unmerged and unreleased, and its immediate-wait false candidate is rejected by active-wait retention. Preliminary execution preceded formal integration, so no blind or held-out claim applies. The original sequential issue history remains inconclusive. These cases establish neither population-wide efficacy nor demand.

Next human-validation gate: five developers outside the project; target three unaided reproductions in about ten minutes and two concrete return visits/questions/cases. Continue expansion after at least one external reproduced regression or repeated use saving work. Those are proposed learning goals, not current traction. No invitations or messages have been sent.

Anthropic/OpenAI support may assist later work. The utility, examples and acceptance checks are independent of subscriptions, credits and provider APIs.
