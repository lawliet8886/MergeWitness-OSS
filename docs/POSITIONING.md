# Positioning and validation boundary

MergeWitness currently replays a caller-authored operation-sequence probe across trusted Git snapshots and checks candidate repairs against frozen explicitly declared requirements. It has a synthetic price/cache proof-and-repair example and a labeled corpus. It does not automatically discover every regression or prove arbitrary merges/repairs safe.

[QuietClash](https://github.com/arbade/quietclash) already compares executable behavior across base, branches and combination; its documentation includes async JS/TS and observability limits. [mumei](https://github.com/iroha924/mumei) reruns reference tests and guards against weakening checks in Claude Code workflows. Frozen checks and behavioral comparison are overlapping ideas, not proprietary categories or evidence of originality.

No executable head-to-head benchmark was run in this implementation. Keep future comparisons confined to supported scenarios, pinned tool versions and published commands; report unsupported and inconclusive cases. The ten-case corpus does not establish a population-wide detection rate, market demand, paid demand or superiority.

The two public DataLoader replays validate a technical ability to replay known sequence-sensitive behavior using unchanged published modules in a minimal harness. They do not establish maintainer adoption or autonomous discovery. One initial observation was corrected against upstream evidence and its held-out label withdrawn; the corpus is not a blind benchmark.

Next human-validation gate: five developers outside the project; target three unaided reproductions in about ten minutes and two concrete return visits/questions/cases. Continue expansion after at least one external reproduced regression or repeated use saving work. Those are proposed learning goals, not current traction. No invitations or messages have been sent.

Anthropic/OpenAI support may assist later work. The utility, examples and acceptance checks are independent of subscriptions, credits and provider APIs.
